WHO WE ARE

A place to belong.
A faith to grow.

Gospel Vision Christian Centre is a church community based in Ogembo. We gather to worship God, grow through His Word, strengthen one another in fellowship, and carry the hope of Christ into our communities.

Under the leadership of Bishop Joseph Ondari Gichana, we welcome you to journey with us in faith.
